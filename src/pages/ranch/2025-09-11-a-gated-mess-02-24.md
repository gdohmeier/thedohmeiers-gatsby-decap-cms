## Lender discussions

We worked with our financial advisor to increase out monthly distribution in preparation for loan applications. We started working with a number of lenders including some folks we had worked with before as well as new ones. We learned that many of the lending companies only wanted to do standard mortgages and would not do land or construction loans. In addition, the ones that did offer construction and land loans all had differing requirements. Different rates, downpayment percent, how interest during construction would be handle. Best advise here is to talk to a bunch of lenders and get all the details you can and pursue the best deals.

We came to the conclusion that the local credit unions (LMCU and MidFlorida) were willing to write loans for construction, and had the best terms. We wanted to go with Joyce (joyce.robitzek@lmcu.org) from LMCU for the construction loan.  We already had our land loan with them and staying with a single lender would simplify the process.  Alas, someone at LMCU decided that they did not to take on the risk of the construction loan and that path was blocked. We eventually did end up with LMCU but that’s for a future post.

We filled out loan application for at least 5 lenders. We had to collect and provide financials, monthly salary, signed construction contract (with GC), plans for the house, and a rough budget; which we did. 

After all was said and done we ended up concentrating on MidFlorida credit union to continue the application process.  In the end LMCO=Gl;;ills
