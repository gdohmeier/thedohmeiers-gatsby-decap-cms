# December 2023

a Proverb I just made up.. Never get over exited or over upset over success, failure or changes to your best layed plans. 

In December of 2023 we decided that we would go on this adventure and build a new house on ten acres and start up a family ranch.  We joined the ‘[Mighty Network](https://actsdereal.mn.co/)’ social media platform so we could chat with other like minded future ranch owners. We started a conversation with my financial advisor to decide how best to proceed financially. I used the ‘ACTS ranch calculator that was available on the website at that time to build out our ranch options and get an idea of the price we were looking at. We stared looking into the type of house we wanted to build this go round, specifically ‘Barndo’ steel buildings. We started working on prequalifying for our land loan with Cross Country Mortgage (was an ACTs recommended lender). At this point we were ready to go all in.

We made another property visit on December 9th to be sure that we were sure about our coming decision.  There property is very beautiful with pastures and groves of trees, numerous man made ponds stocked with tilapia fish, beef cattle and wild horses. The most amazing thing that both Sue and I realized about this place was how wonderfully quiet it was, no road noises, no hustle and bustle, just quiet. There is also a pretty nice constant breeze due to the lack of structures and cement everywhere like there is in the city or even suburbs. After this visit we were basically decided.

Reservation 

On or about December 12, 2023 we put in our ‘Parcel reservation fee’ of $2500 dollars to reserve our spot to buy land at ‘Panoplia Ranch’ in Highlands County Florida, about a hour and forty minutes from where we where currently living in 
Valrico.  We had our Land Stake Holder (zoom) meeting on Dec 14th and met with TJ, the CEO and founder of ACTs Decentralized Real Estate. Our meeting with TJ was good  and we were able to learn about him and he about us. Our plans were to sell our current house (after the first of the year), buy our 10 acres and build asap. We liked the idea of starting a ranch with cows, pigs and chickens; but that was always our secondary goal to getting our ‘family compound’ started.
