## ACTs year end update

We began January with an exciting year end update from ACTs announcing road improvements, parcel selections, and a January 29th or sooner closing for Panoplia.

![Email](/img/img_0725.jpg "Year end update")


## Home Financing, Sale and Parcel selection

We spoke to our realtor and got a valuation for the sale of our current home; our plan was to sell our home purchase our land and immediately move to construction. The home market was in a good place for selling and our house had appreciated. We found a mortgage broker that we liked and even where looking into a HELOC if we could not sell our quick enough to close. We had pre-qualified with a couple of mortgage lenders for our loan and were basically looking for the best rate. At this point we were trying to see if we could go directly to a land/construction loan and working with ACTs & their partners to see if that was a possibility. At this point ACTs had a partner for construction but they were not prepared to go directly to construction. Also the closing details we needed for our lenders where not readily available, so we were constrained to just buying the land.

By mid January we had prepped our house for sale (A lot of quick work) and were nearly ready to list. Due to the pressure to close by the end of January, we were working hard to get our home sold. We had a plan to move in with our daughter and son-in-law once we sold and live there during the build. (*As I’m writing this blog post its Sat Aug 30th 2025 and we just received our building permit. We are so thankful and praise God that we have are permit now and begin our build. The astute reader will recognize that its a year and a half since the month this post is describing… stay tuned for the rest of the months to see all the ups and downs…).* 

By the end of January we had loan preapproval and I had begun researching different solar providers seeking information and quote. We received an email that we were up next for selecting our parcel - we had an extended discussion, but decided on parcel 79 (which turned out to be a good choice).

## On Jan 31st

* We chose our parcel, and learned we are obviously not closing on Jan 29th, more on that later.
* We locked down our financing, we thought.
* We set up ‘success call’ with ACTs to discuss our financing
* We got acceptance on a contract for the sale of our house!

Crazy…

## Home Design

At this point in our journey we were convinced that we wanted a Barndominium. We were looking at design websites and contacting steel building vendors. We found a style that we liked best was the Modern Farmhouse. A single story three bedroom open concept inside with porches on the front and back. I had begun to research the solar capacity that we would need and began to reach out to companies for cost estimates. Below is an example of that the type of houses we were looking at.
